Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Cyber Global Technologies LLC

The short version: cyberglobal.ai itself is a marketing and advisory site — it does not process AI workloads or collect sensitive data through a product. It does run basic visitor analytics (page views, referrer, a randomly generated visitor ID) to understand traffic. Our individual products (Compliance AI, PropAudit, OutreachSafe, InboxSafe, VendorSafe, ReportSafe, and others) each have their own privacy policy covering their specific data handling, including any AI processing — this page covers cyberglobal.ai and Cyber Global Technologies LLC as the parent entity.

1. Who We Are

Cyber Global Technologies LLC ("CGT", "we", "us", or "our") is a compliance and IT risk advisory firm based in New York, and the parent company of the products listed on this site. You can contact us at info@cyberglobal.ai or (929) 368-4181.

2. What We Collect on cyberglobal.ai

  • Basic visitor analytics — page path, referrer, approximate country (derived from your connection, not GPS), browser user agent, and screen width, recorded against a randomly generated visitor ID and session ID stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Campaign parameters — UTM source/medium/campaign/content values, if you arrive via a tracked link, persisted for the length of your session to attribute conversions.
  • Consultation requests — if you contact us via this site, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message) to respond to your inquiry.

We do not use cookies for this tracking — the visitor/session identifiers are stored in localStorage/sessionStorage and are specific to your browser, not shared across devices.

3. Analytics Infrastructure

Visitor analytics events are sent to a Supabase-hosted database that we operate, shared across the Cyber Global Technologies product portfolio for aggregate traffic reporting. This data is used internally for marketing and product analytics only — we do not sell it, and it is not shared with any product's AI provider or third party beyond our own analytics infrastructure.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

Analytics events are retained indefinitely in aggregate for traffic reporting; individual visitor/session identifiers are not linked to a real identity unless you separately contact us. Consultation request data (name, email, message) is retained while relevant to an active inquiry and deleted on request.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data we hold about you by emailing info@cyberglobal.ai.

7. Our Products

Each product under Cyber Global Technologies — including Compliance AI, PropAudit, OutreachSafe, InboxSafe, VendorSafe, ReportSafe, MergeMind, and Thunderbird Organizer — has its own privacy policy specific to that product's data handling and any AI processing involved. Consult the individual product's site for details.

8. Security

All data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Access to analytics and consultation data is restricted to authorized Cyber Global Technologies personnel.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
